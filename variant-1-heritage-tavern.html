Our Story

"London's oldest markets, pubs and stories — all tasted, not just seen."

For more than a decade we've been walking small groups through the bits of London the guidebooks miss. Our guides aren't actors — they're cooks, brewers, history nerds and lifelong Londoners who happen to be excellent company.

Every tasting is hand-picked from family-run producers. Every story is true. Every pub has a fireplace.

Traditional English
Food Tour.

⏱3.5 Hours 📍London Bridge → Borough Market ☻Max 12 Guests ★4.9 (312 reviews)
The Experience

A walking feast through 1,000 years of London.

Begin at the foot of London Bridge, where Roman ships once docked. Wind through Bermondsey backstreets to a Dickensian pie shop, taste cheeses from the same stall trusted since 1742, and finish with ale in a tavern that's poured pints since the year of the Great Fire.

Your guide is a born-and-raised Londoner with stories that won't appear in any guidebook — and an opinion on every cheese.

The Itinerary
✦
01
11:00 · Meeting Point
London Bridge Station

Welcome, introductions, a glass of bubbly to start, and a quick history of how this very bridge built modern London.

02
11:25 · Stop One
Borough Market — Cheesemonger

A guided tasting of three of England's greatest cheeses with the Borough family who's sold them since 1742.

03
12:00 · Stop Two
The Old Pie Shop

Steak & ale pie with mash, gravy and mushy peas in a tavern Dickens drank in. Yes, that one.

04
12:50 · Stop Three
Maltby Street Market

Smoked salt beef bagels, craft cider, and a stop at the city's smallest gin distillery.

05
13:45 · Final Stop
The George Inn — Last Galleried Tavern

Real ale, sticky toffee pudding, and the closing chapter of London's oldest food street.
